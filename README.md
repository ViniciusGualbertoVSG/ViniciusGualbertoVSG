### 🚀 What I'm building

**[n8x](https://github.com/Grupo-Vannuchi/n8x)** — Full-service marketing agency platform in Next.js 16. The entire brand is driven by a single config file, and the bilingual UI is editable from an authenticated admin panel. Case portfolio, stats, testimonials, and lead capture for both contact and careers. My largest contribution to date — 86 commits.

**[MetodoChat](https://github.com/Grupo-Vannuchi/DirectPro-V1.5.1)** — Instagram DM automation on Meta's official Graph API. A keyword in a comment, reel or story triggers an automatic DM with a link, operating within the platform's 24-hour messaging window instead of working around it. Serverless on Neon and Upstash QStash.

**On-demand services platform** *(private, at work)* — Turborepo monorepo connecting customers to verified service providers, supporting both scheduled and emergency dispatch with end-to-end traceability of each job.

**[Inventory Hub](https://github.com/ViniciusGualbertoVSG/inventory-hub-web)** — Multi-tenant inventory SaaS for small businesses. I built the frontend: route protection centralized in middleware with JWT signature verification, explicit active-company context for tenant isolation, and role-based access separating `OWNER` from `EMPLOYEE`. Backend in NestJS by a project partner.

### 🔬 Beyond the web stack

**Network security** — Undergraduate thesis on penetration testing: a comparative study and application of the OSSTMM, NIST SP 800-115 and PTES methodologies, carried out in an isolated virtual lab under faculty supervision. Traffic analysis with Wireshark, vulnerability assessment with Nessus.

**Computer vision & deep learning** — Replaced a classical CV pipeline (hand-crafted descriptors with SVM/KNN) with neural networks for plant species classification on the Flavia dataset — 1,907 images across 32 species. U-Net encoder-decoder for semantic segmentation, then CNN and MobileNetV2 transfer learning for classification, reaching **98% accuracy**.

**Undergraduate research** — Research scholarship at UTFPR building a learning-styles assessment platform based on the Honey-Alonso and Kolb methodologies.
